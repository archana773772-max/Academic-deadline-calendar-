About the Project

The Academic Deadline Calendar is a student-friendly website that manages assignments, examinations, practicals and other academic activities in one place.

Why Is It Important?

  • Helps students remember important deadlines.
  • Improves time management and planning.
  • Organizes tasks according to courses.
  • Reduces the chances of missing submissions.

Deadlines within the next 3 days will display a reminder. Past tasks that are not completed will be marked overdue.

How It Works

  1. Enter the task name and subject.
  2. Select the activity, priority, deadline date and time.
  3. Click Add Deadline.
  4. See it on the monthly calendar and in your deadline list.
  5. Mark tasks as done, or search, filter, edit and delete them.
  6. Export your list as CSV or as a calendar (.ics) file.
  7. Make a JSON backup and restore it later if browser data is lost.
  8. Turn on notifications and dark mode from the top of the page.
